# Users and roles

Access is managed per project.

## Roles

- Viewer: sees records and exports.
- Collector: fills forms on the device, sees their own records.
- Editor: sees and edits all records, resolves conflicts.
- Admin: everything, plus members, forms and project settings.

## Adding a member

Open the project, then Members, then Add. Enter the person's email and pick a role. They get an invitation by email.

## Removing a member

Removing a member keeps their records. Their device stops syncing on the next attempt.

## Organization admins

Organization admins can create projects and manage templates. Project roles do not grant organization rights.
