# Building forms

A form is a list of fields. Each record filled in the field is one row of data.

## Field types

- Text, number, date and time
- Choice (one or many) with a fixed list of options
- Photo, with optional GPS position and compass bearing
- Location, captured from the device GPS or placed on the map
- Signature

## Field settings

Every field has a label, a short name used in exports, a required flag and a help text shown under the field.

## Templates

Save any form as a template to reuse it in other projects. Templates live at organization level. To use one, choose New form and pick it from the template list.

## Form versions

Changing a form creates a new version. Records keep the version they were made with. Exports include a version column.
