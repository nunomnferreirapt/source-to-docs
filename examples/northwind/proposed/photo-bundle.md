# Photo bundle

A photo bundle is a zip file with every photo in a project, or in a filtered set of records.

## Make a bundle

1. In the web app, open Project, then Exports, then Photo bundle.
2. Filter by form or date range if you do not want all records.
3. Start the bundle. Large bundles take a few minutes to prepare.

You get an email with a download link when the bundle is ready. The link opens for the person who requested the bundle, so you may be asked to sign in again.

## What is inside

One folder per form. Each photo is named after the record id and the field short name.

## Limits

- A bundle holds up to 20 GB per request. Above that, filter by form or date range and make more than one bundle.
- The download link works for 7 days.
- Limits are the same for all plans.
